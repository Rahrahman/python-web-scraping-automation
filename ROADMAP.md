## 📋 বিস্তারিত মডিউল

- [ ] 01. Variables, Data Types & Operators

- [ ] 05. Functions, Scope, `*args`, `**kwargs`, Lambda
- [ ] 06. List/Dict Comprehensions

- [ ] 07. Classes, Objects, Methods, `__init__`
- [ ] 08. OOP 4 Pillars: Encapsulation, Inheritance, Polymorphism, Abstraction

### 🟠 Phase 5: Industry Specialization (Backend & APIs)

### 🔴 Phase 6: Job Hunt & Portfolio Building
- [ ] 24. Production-grade Portfolio Projects
- [ ] 25. Mock Technical Coding Interviews & Take-Home Assignments
- [ ] 26. Remote Job Profiles (LinkedIn, Upwork, Wellfound/AngelList, Arc.dev)
